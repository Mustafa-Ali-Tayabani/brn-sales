Guardrails

  • Every send checks the do-not-contact list, lawful basis, local business hours (Sun–Thu in the Gulf, Mon–Fri elsewhere; calls 10:00–17:00) and daily caps.
  • Every email has an unsubscribe link and your postal address. A "stop" reply or a "remove me" on a call adds the lead to the do-not-contact list for good.
  • AI calls to US/Canada numbers only happen with prior opt-in (TCPA).
  • Leads with no email or phone wait in "needs contact" until you add one.
  • Live mode stays locked until the server sets BRN_ALLOW_LIVE=1.
